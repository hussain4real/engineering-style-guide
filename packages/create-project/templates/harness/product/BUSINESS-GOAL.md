# Business Goal — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

> Fill this first. Keep it to one page. This is the *why* every later decision points back to.
> Tip (Claude Code): ask the PM agent to interview you through these questions, then write your
> answers here — see the `discovery-to-prd` playbook.

## 1. The objective (one sentence)
_What outcome are we trying to create? Not the feature — the outcome._

## 2. Who is it for
_The specific user/role and the job they're trying to get done._

## 3. Why now / status quo
_How is this handled today (Excel, email, a legacy system)? What's the pain that makes change worth it?_

## 4. The wedge (narrowest first slice)
_The smallest thing we can ship that is genuinely useful on its own._

## 5. Success metrics
_How will we know it worked? 1–3 measurable signals (e.g. time saved, error rate, adoption)._

## 6. Constraints & non-goals
_Regulatory, security, budget, timeline constraints. What we are explicitly NOT doing._

## 7. Time horizon
_When does this need to deliver value? What's the decision/demo date that anchors the plan?_

## 8. Stakeholders
_Who decides, who is consulted, who owns the outcome._
